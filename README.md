# Flipkart Sales Dashboard 📊

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Flipkart sales data to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, payment preferences, and regional distribution.
An interactive dashboard was created using Excel/Power BI to help visualize trends, monitor KPIs, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Track sales performance across products, categories, and regions
* Identify seasonal trends and high-sales periods
* Analyze customer ratings and satisfaction levels
* Understand payment method preferences
* Evaluate regional sales distribution and growth opportunities
* Provide actionable insights for business strategy

---

## Key Features

* Interactive Sales Dashboard
* KPI Cards for Total Sales, Orders, Ratings, and Revenue
* Region-wise Sales Analysis
* Category & Product Performance Tracking
* Customer Rating Analysis
* Payment Method Insights
* Monthly & Seasonal Sales Trends
* Dynamic Charts and Filters

## Insights Generated

* Identified top-performing product categories
* Discovered seasonal spikes during sales campaigns/festivals
* Analyzed customer satisfaction trends
* Found preferred payment methods among customers
* Compared regional sales performance for growth analysis
